TiviMate on Android TV: every step in order (2026)

✍️ Hannah Morgan · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 4 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

This guide shows you how to install and configure TiviMate on Android TV step by step. You will need a paid IPTV subscription like SubTV IP to start streaming.

Follow each step in order to set up your device in 2026.

What You Need

  • An Android TV device (Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Xiaomi Mi Box S, Android TV Box, or similar)
  • A paid IPTV subscription – for example, SubTV IP from subtvip.store
  • A stable internet connection
  • Your TV remote control or a wireless keyboard

Step-by-Step Setup

  1. Enable installation from unknown sources (only needed if you sideload TiviMate or use Downloader). Go to Settings > Device Preferences > Security & Restrictions > Unknown Sources and turn it on.
  2. Install TiviMate from the Google Play Store on your Android TV. Search for “TiviMate” and press Install. If you prefer sideloading, use the Downloader app and enter the official TiviMate APK URL.
  3. Open TiviMate after installation. You will see a start screen asking for a playlist.
  4. Add your playlist – go to Settings > Playlist > Add Playlist > M3U URL. Enter the unique playlist URL you received from your provider (e.g., SubTV IP gives you this after purchase).
  5. Add the EPG (Electronic Program Guide) – go to Settings > EPG > Add EPG > M3U URL and paste the EPG URL from your provider. TiviMate will fetch the guide data.
  6. Adjust the buffer size to prevent freezing – go to Settings > Playback > Buffer size and set it to 5000 ms.
  7. Change your DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) on the Android TV system level – go to Settings > Network & Internet > Wi‑Fi > (your network) > IP settings > DNS and enter 1.1.1.1 (or 1.0.0.1 as secondary). This helps avoid ISP throttling.
  8. Restart TiviMate and start watching. Your channels and guide should appear within a few seconds.

If a Step Fails

  • Playlist not loading? Double-check the URL. It must be exact – copy it from your provider’s dashboard.
  • Buffer still freezes? Ensure you set the buffer to 5000 ms and that Cloudflare DNS is active. Also verify your internet speed (at least 25 Mbps for 4K).
  • Can’t find TiviMate in Play Store? Your device may be region‑locked. Use the Downloader app with the official TiviMate website URL.
  • EPG missing? Some providers use Xtream Codes – in TiviMate select “Xtream Codes API” instead of M3U URL and enter the portal address, username and password.

Frequently Asked Questions about TiviMate on Android TV

Does TiviMate work on Android TV in 2026?

Yes. TiviMate version 5.x is fully compatible with Android TV 12 and later, including Fire TV, Xiaomi Mi Box S, and most Android TV boxes.

How do I get a playlist URL for TiviMate?

You must subscribe to a paid IPTV service. After ordering from SubTV IP, you receive a unique M3U link in your account dashboard – use that URL in TiviMate.

Is there an instant activation for TiviMate or the IPTV subscription?

No. TiviMate offers a free version with limited features, but the premium features (like EPG, multi‑screen) require a one‑time payment within the app. The IPTV subscription from SubTV IP is a paid service with instant activation – no free trial is available.

How do I set Cloudflare DNS on Android TV?

Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Wi‑Fi > (your network) > IP settings > Static. Change DNS 1 to 1.1.1.1 and DNS 2 to 1.0.0.1. Save the settings and reconnect.

Why is my stream buffering even after setup?

First, set the buffer size in TiviMate to 5000 ms. Then make sure Cloudflare DNS is active. If the problem continues, check your internet speed – 4K streams need at least 25 Mbps. Restart your router and Android TV device.

Can I use TiviMate on Fire TV Stick 4K Max?

Yes. The Fire TV Stick 4K Max runs Android TV, so you can install TiviMate from the Play Store or sideload it. Follow the same steps above.

How do I update TiviMate?

Open the Google Play Store on your Android TV, go to “My apps & games”, find TiviMate, and press Update. For sideloaded versions, download the latest APK from the official site.
